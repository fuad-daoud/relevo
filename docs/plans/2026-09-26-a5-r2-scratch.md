# A5 R2: restore the scratch worktree primitive

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-09-24-cockpit-design.md` D6 and §3.4.

A reader round runs in a **scratch worktree**: a detached copy of the binding's HEAD
plus its whole working state (tracked edits, deletions and untracked non-ignored files),
discarded when the round closes, so the binding's own tree is never touched. The
original design is `docs/plans/2026-09-24-cockpit-a5a-scratch-worktree.md`; read it.

That code existed and was deleted as unused by cleanup commit `25734a04` (#582). **This
round restores it, adapted to today's code.** R4 wires it into reader rounds.

**If a step is impossible as written or contradicts the code, stop and report. Do not
improvise, and do not bend a test to fit.**
