# Plan T6: tmp-usage

Part of the disk lane (#1314 runner temp dirs, #1308 Go build cache).

Depends on T2.

**Behaviour.** A pure, bounded measurement of the relevo tmp root. It reports the bytes under `rounds/`, `checks/` and loose entries, with a truncation flag, and builds a `doctor.Check` from the numbers. Nothing is wired into `relevo doctor` here. C5 owns `cmd/relevo/doctor.go`.

**Seams.**
- New `internal/relevo/tmpusage.go`:
  - `TmpUsageOf(root string, budget time.Duration, maxEntries int) TmpUsage`.
  - A bounded walk that stops at the time or entry budget and sets `Truncated`. It does not follow symlinks.
- New `internal/doctor/disk.go`:
  - `TmpRootCheck(rounds, checks, loose int64, truncated bool, warnBytes int64) Check`.
  - Plain integers in, so `internal/doctor` does not import `relevo`.
  - Severity is warn above the threshold, with a Fix that says what is swept and how to clear it. The default threshold is 10 GiB, a named constant.

**Steps.**
1. Add the walk and its tests, then the check builder and its tests. Deliverable: `go test ./internal/relevo -run TmpUsage ./internal/doctor/...` is green.
2. Run `make check-affected`.
3. Save the plan to `docs/plans/2026-10-23-tmp-usage.md` in the same commit.

**Tests.**
- `TestTmpUsageSplitsRoundsChecksLoose`.
- `TestTmpUsageStopsAtBudget`, with an injected clock or entry cap.
- `TestTmpUsageDoesNotFollowSymlinks`.
- `TestTmpRootCheckWarnsOverThreshold`, and the not-warning case at the threshold.
- `TestTmpRootCheckMarksTruncated`.

**Mutation targets.**
- Remove the budget stop: the budget test must fail.
- Follow symlinks: the symlink test must fail.
- Change `>` to `>=` at the threshold: the boundary test must fail.

**Deleted.** None.

**Report must include.**
- The exported names C5 must call.
