# Cockpit A2, round 3: `--actor`, and the words catch up

Rounds 1 and 2 are on this branch and were verified. Round 2 was also run against a
copy of a real machine's config: it migrated cleanly, and the picks were unchanged.
This round finishes A2's user-facing surface.

**If a step is impossible as written or contradicts the code, stop and report. Do not
improvise.** CI has no harness and no network. No `cmd/relevo` test may run `bind`,
`ask` or `send`, because they spawn harnesses. Test flag handling through pure
helpers or `parseFlags`-level tests only. **Plain text only:** no ANSI escapes in any
`internal/relevo` or `cmd/relevo` output.

**Out of scope:** the binding's stored `Role` field, `BindingStatus.Role`'s JSON key
(`role`), and the wire's `CreateBindingRequest.Role` all keep their names. A4
renames state and wire. This round changes flags and words only.
