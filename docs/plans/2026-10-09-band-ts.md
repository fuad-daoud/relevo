## Behaviour and cases

- Poller from identity-resolve on: `relevo status --line --json` with the mod's resolved env every 5 s via `$.clock.every`; skip while a run is in flight (no overlap); after 3 consecutive failures back off to 30 s until one success; store the document in a `$.state`/`atom` value; readers redraw on write; failed/stale poll keeps the last doc.
- Inbox rows: bindings waiting on the MasterMind, oldest first by `last_ts`, at most 3 then `+N more · /relevo:status`; one line each: digit, `● <name> r<round>`, `<reason> · <age>`, then the halt reason (not question text, per #952 comment) or `delivered · +A/-R in F` from `live`; inbox rule uses `tone` `needs`/`report` (builder states why vs `needs_you`/`report_in`, noting the `Display` override edge below).
- Rail line, always when resolved with bindings: `relevo · <mastermind name>` then every binding NOT in the inbox as `○ <name> <activity>` where `<activity>` is the row's `status` (already carries the activity word in flight); chain rows render the `chain` string as-is with no bar when structured progress is absent; a binding is in the inbox or on the rail, never both.
- `next(e)` (engine's own row, draw nothing of ours) when no bindings, while `e.props.hasSurvey`, or when `relevo` missing/identity never resolved.
- Size to `e.props.bodyColumns`; truncate (`truncate-end`), never wrap; keep the tree within `maxRows`.
- Row/rail item as `Button` (`plain`, `hotkey` digit, `ctrl+x tab` + digit, click): `$.prompt.fill({ text: 'relevo show <name> --report' })` as a draft, never send; pane comes later (#953).
- No toasts, no pane, no new commands, no change to delivery behaviour.
- Cases: no bindings; calm (all rail); one waiting + one running (never both); 4+ waiting (`+N more`); stale/failed poll keeps last; missing binary draws nothing; chain row; missing new fields degrade (no word / no bar). Mutation: inbox bindings duplicated on the rail must fail the never-both test.
