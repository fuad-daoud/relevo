# A4-3: the wire says actor, runner and candidate

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-09-26-a4-state-rename-design.md`, §2 and §3.5. It is a **clean
break** (D1): the server and client ship in one release, and neither accepts or sends an
old field or feature token. Internal Go names stay (D3): only JSON tags, feature token
**values** and printed words change.

**Vocabulary:**
- an **actor** is config;
- a **runner** is the live process and plays one actor;
- a **candidate** is the model a round runs on.

**If a step is impossible as written or contradicts the code, stop and report. Do not
improvise, and do not bend a test to fit.**

**Owned elsewhere, do not edit:**
- `internal/store/*` and `internal/view/*` (A4-2a, open);
- the architect copies and `handoff.md`.
