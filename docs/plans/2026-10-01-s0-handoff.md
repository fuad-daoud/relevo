count`. Seen on reviewer rounds 1, 2 and 3 and on security round 1 across runs;
the test also passes sometimes.

Relevant code: `internal/relevo/headless.go:holdReaderOnMarker` (holds a reader
round on its marker until the process exits, 2 min grace) and
`internal/relevo/reconcile.go:closeOnMarker` -> `writeReaderSummary`, which
reads the round's **stream** (`<binding>/NNN-runner.jsonl`, unchanged by S0). An
empty stream there yields "without a report" and no verdict.

`StreamPath`/`ReadFile` miss) while the fake harness was still between writing
its marker and printing its JSON message (the fake sleeps 0.2 s), so the round
closed before the stream carried the message. The alternative is a genuinely
empty stream from the supervisor.
